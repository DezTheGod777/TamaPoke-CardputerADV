TamaPoke Cardputer ADV v0.9.0
Hardware status: APPROVED on physical M5Stack Cardputer ADV
Public firmware filename: TamaPoke-CardputerADV-v0.9.0.bin

Major v0.9.0 additions:
- Persistent personality traits and live moods
- Richer idle behavior and species/time/habitat reactions
- Home customization with selectable Meadow, Beach, Forest, Volcano, Mountain, Snow, Starfield and Dream backgrounds
- Inventory, Poke Shop and coin economy
- Six additional minigames with persistent high scores
- Daily Life rewards, greetings, care bonuses, visitors and anniversary tracking
- Deeper Pokedex history and favorites
- Three-slot Save Manager with integrity verification and staged restore
- Event-only audio upgrade with cries, jingles and care/evolution sounds; no looping background music/ambience
- Animation polish, shiny effects, evolution presentation and sleep effects
- Clock / Calendar with 12-hour time, seconds, weekday, full date and original Gastly/Haunter/Gengar PMD sprites
- Manual date/time editor and one-shot Wi-Fi/NTP time sync with saved-network support
- Starfield and Dream are normal selectable backgrounds; the retired 151 border is not included
- Mystery Gift / Mew Visitor / Ultra Shiny secret content remains optional

Compatibility:
- Pet save journal remains compatible with v0.7 /tamapoke_v7_a.bin and /tamapoke_v7_b.bin
- Original time-based leveling remains intact: 1 real minute = 1 in-game minute, +1 level per real hour
- Original evolution level/path requirements remain intact; care mistakes can delay evolution
- Original sunrise/day/sunset/night thresholds remain intact
- Existing v0.8.5.4 display, battery, event-history and clean terrarium behavior remains included

Development history remains on branch ultimate-v0.9.0 until the release PR is explicitly merged.
