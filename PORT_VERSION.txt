TamaPoke Cardputer ADV v0.9.0 Ultimate
Active development branch: ultimate-v0.9.0
Phase 1: real personality foundation
Phase 2: complete mood system and temperament-driven care behavior
Phase 3: much richer idle life and visible habitat/species reactions
Traits: PLAYFUL, BOLD, GENTLE, CALM, CURIOUS, STUBBORN, LAZY, AFFECTIONATE, ENERGETIC, SHY
Moods: CONTENT, EXCITED, BORED, SLEEPY, HUNGRY, LONELY, PROUD, ANNOYED, SICK, CURIOUS, AFFECTIONATE, DIRTY
Traits are derived from values already present in the v0.7 pet journal, so they survive evolution/restart without changing save compatibility
Home header reports trait / mood during normal healthy play; urgent care states use direct messages such as hungry, sick, lonely or annoyed
Phase 3 idle life includes broader wandering, visible short dozes, sky-watching, stretch-like moments, sits/hops, morning/night reactions, habitat-condition reactions and occasional foreground check-ins
Foreground check-ins briefly enlarge and center the Pokemon to make affectionate/curious approaches feel closer to the player
Expanded species-family behaviors now cover starter families, common bird lines, Jigglypuff, Psyduck, Abra, Eevee/original evolutions, Ditto and legendary birds in addition to Phase 2 signature species
Habitat reactions respond to the firmware's visible snow/leaves/shore/embers/night-sky conditions; they do not use live internet weather
Trait subtly influences live and offline need-decay rates without making any temperament overwhelmingly better or worse
No looping background audio ambience/music is added
Existing visual habitat effects, stable Home HUD fixes, display controls, event history and clean terrarium mode remain intact
Pet save format remains compatible with v0.7 /tamapoke_v7_a.bin and /tamapoke_v7_b.bin
